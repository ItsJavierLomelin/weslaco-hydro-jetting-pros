Local area 01

Downtown Weslaco

Downtown Weslaco was drawn up in 1919 by investors who wanted a town between Donna and Mercedes.

Local area 02

Camp Llano Grande Area

In 1916 the army built Camp Llano Grande just east of what is now downtown Weslaco, and 12,000 soldiers trained there for World War I.

Local area 03

Gibson Park and Valley Nature Center

Gibson Park began in 1934 when the Weslaco Lions Club set up a park, and the Valley Nature Center now occupies its eastern portion.
