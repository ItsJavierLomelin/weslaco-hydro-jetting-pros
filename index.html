Hydro jetting or snaking?

Mechanical rodding pushes a bladed rod through a blockage. Jetting directs pressurized water at the debris. Which one fits depends on the obstruction and the pipe.

Weslaco's restaurants and shops downtown put grease on lines from the 1919 townsite. A kitchen line that keeps slowing down may need more than a path punched through the blockage. Tell us about heavy kitchen use, food waste and how long the last cleaning held.

Hydro Jetting for Tree Roots

Trees on Weslaco's older streets and park edges raise a fair root question for lines that old. Root trouble needs two answers: how to clear the line and whether the pipe has a defect that let the roots in. Tell us where roots were found and what happened afterward.

A drain that keeps slowing in Weslaco may sit on a 1919 downtown line or on a newer subdivision line. A drain that slows again after cleaning needs a fresh look. Note how long the improvement lasted and whether one fixture or several are affected.

Hydro Jetting for Mineral Buildup

Hard deposits are a question about the pipe wall, so in Weslaco the first step is to identify the material. A slow drain does not mean a hard deposit is present. A qualified professional should identify the material and check the pipe before choosing a cleaning method.

Preventative Hydro Jetting

Weslaco's hot summers and heavy September rain make a written record of past trouble useful. Plan cleaning around a record of past drain trouble, not a fixed rule that every pipe needs the same schedule. Keep notes on repeat blockages and any inspection findings.
